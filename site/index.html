Why Googol Vibe?

Unified Dashboard

Gmail, Calendar, Drive, and Tasks in one cohesive interface. No more tab juggling.

Optional AI assistant

Ask about your mail, calendar, files and tasks with your own Anthropic API key. Off until you add one.

Privacy First

No CodeTonight server sees your data. The app talks to Google from your Mac, and the AI assistant and error reports stay off until you turn them on.
